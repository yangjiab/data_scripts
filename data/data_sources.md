# Data Sources

## Atmospheric Data

Atmospheric forcing data used in this study were obtained from the National Cryosphere Desert Data Center of China:

http://www.ncdc.ac.cn

## Cropland Data

Cropland distribution datasets were obtained from the National Earth System Science Data Center of China:

http://www.nesdc.org.cn

## Agricultural Record Data

Agricultural statistical records were collected from the Hebei Statistical Yearbook:

http://tjj.hebei.gov.cn/hbstjj/sj/sjcx/tjnj/

## Groundwater Observation Data

Groundwater observation data are subject to policy restrictions and cannot be publicly shared in their original form.

## Other Data

The administrative boundary map of China used in this study was approved under the cartographic review number:

GS(2019)1822
